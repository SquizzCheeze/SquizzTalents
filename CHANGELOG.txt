SquizzTalents - Changelog
=========================


V0.4
----

New:

- Release notes window, once per update, like the other Squizz addons. It
  shares their queue, so notes from several addons updating at once appear one
  after another instead of on top of each other. /sqt notes shows them again.

Changed:

- The reminder's Remember control is a dropdown instead of a button that cycled
  through options. It opens on the mapping that actually triggered the reminder
  (it used to say "off" even when "All dungeons" was saved), and every option
  names the build it currently points at.

Fixed:

- Entering a delve could miss the reminder: the game had not reported the delve
  yet when the check ran, 3 seconds after loading. The check now retries at 8 and
  15 seconds until the content is recognised, and /sqt debug records what the
  game reported at each attempt.


V0.3
----

New:

- Saved builds made on an older talent tree are marked Outdated, with the reason
  in the tooltip. Checked from the build's header only, so it costs nothing.
- Update to current talents (right-click): overwrites a saved build with your
  active talents and keeps its name, icon, tags and content mappings.
- Delete outdated: removes every outdated saved build for your spec at once.
- Blizzard loadouts can be deleted from the right-click menu. Blizzard's talent
  window only notices while it is open; if it still lists a deleted loadout,
  /reload.
- /sqt testoutdated saves a deliberately outdated copy of your build, for
  testing the above without waiting for a patch.


V0.2
----

New:

- Reminder popup on entering a dungeon, raid, delve, battleground or arena, on
  slotting a keystone, and on a ready check. It only appears when your active
  build is not the one chosen for that content, never during an active key, and
  waits for combat to end.
- Content mappings: one difficulty of an instance, the whole instance, or every
  dungeon / raid / delve / battleground / arena. Set from the reminder or by
  right-clicking a build.
- Right-click menu: apply, use for this content, tags, icon, rename, delete.
- Settings page (Options > AddOns > SquizzTalents, or /sqt config): reminder
  switches and the mapping list for your spec.
- /sqt remind runs the reminder check on demand.


V0.1
----

- First version: one list of your Blizzard loadouts and your own saved builds,
  with no cap on how many you save. Click to apply; the result is confirmed by
  the game rather than assumed. /sqt debug for a copyable diagnostic dump.
